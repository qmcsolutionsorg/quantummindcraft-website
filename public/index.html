One brain for your whole life

Twelve powerful worlds, beautifully connected. Each one deep enough to replace a dedicated app — together they replace them all.

Health & Wellness

Separate medical records for every family member — even pets. Vaccinations, prescriptions, BP & sugar tracking, reports, tests. A lifesaver in emergencies.

Finance & Accounting

Bank accounts, cards, loans, wallets, payables & receivables — plus real balance sheets with assets, liabilities and equity for personal and business.

Business & ERP

Run multiple businesses separately. Quotations → invoices → payments, products, partners, P&L statements. A mini-ERP that stays simple.

Employees & Workforce

Attendance, login/logout, working hours, salaries, tasks and approvals for staff, workers or household help — a smart operations system inside the app.

School & Learning

Subjects, assignments, projects, sports, music classes, deadlines and events — so no teacher email or project ever slips through again.

Parenting & Family

Shared activities, routines, reminders and built-in approvals. Structured messaging to stay connected — even across distance or complex situations.

Mom's Diary

Pregnancy & cycle tracking, milestones, appointments and reports — with separate records for each pregnancy and child. A personal life companion.

Asset Management

Houses, vehicles, gold, equipment — with documents, warranties, servicing, insurance, appreciation/depreciation. A digital life-history for everything you own.

Payables

Subscriptions, bills, rent, salaries, vendor payments — with custom categories and separate visibility per business. Never miss a payment again.

Trips & Travel

Separate trips with schedules, tickets, bookings, documents, checklists and expenses — personal or business. Know exactly what each trip cost.

Documents

Reports, invoices, contracts, receipts, IDs — all uploaded and instantly findable. No more digging through chats and galleries.

Collaborations

Keep every partnership separate — members, goals, documents, budgets and progress — for business, marketing, events or social causes.

Your own space for projects, goals, dreams and wishlists — with documentation, deadlines, budgets and visible progress that keeps you motivated.
