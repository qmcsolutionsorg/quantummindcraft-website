Everything QuantrolPlus covers

Organize

Notes, contacts, your daily planner, what you have lent out and what is coming up — the everyday admin that otherwise lives on scraps of paper.

  • Projects — Plan and track personal projects end to end — tasks, deadlines, budgets, documents and progress you can actually see.
  • Vacations — Plan trips with itineraries, budgets and checklists, then track what you actually spend along the way.
  • Notes — Capture ideas, journals and quick notes in rich text — organised and always with you.
  • Contacts — Keep the people in your life in one place, with the details and shared history that matter.
  • Daily Habbits
  • Upcoming Events
  • My Daily Planner
  • Library
  • Lend and borrow

Family

Kids, parents and grandparents together — school, money, tasks, approvals and the conversations that hold it all together, even across distance.

  • Parenting — Built for long-distance and separated parenting — plans, approvals, handovers and messages, all around the child.
  • Shared Parenting Finances — Who paid for what, what is owed, and what still needs agreeing — kept clear between both households.
  • Parenting and approvals — Approve your kids permissions, purchases and decisions — stay informed without hovering.
  • New folder

Business

Run several businesses side by side — partners, leads, staff, sales, stock and accounts, each kept cleanly separate.

  • Multiple businesses — Run several businesses from one app, each with its own books, team and dashboard.
  • Business partners — Manage customers, vendors and clients in one place, with every transaction linked back to them.
  • Leads and CRM — Track leads through your pipeline from first contact to closed deal.
  • Employee management — Manage staff, roles, timesheets, leave and payroll — all in one place.
  • Sales flow — Quotation to Sales Order to Delivery to Invoice to Payment, with every step posted to your books.
  • Inventory — Track raw materials, finished goods and services — stock levels, costs and valuation, always current.
  • Invoices — Raise invoices from a sales order or a product, track payments and tax, and see what is still outstanding.
  • Chart of Account — A full, real double-entry chart of accounts behind every transaction you make.
  • Purchases — Buy from vendors the same way you sell — Purchase Order to Goods Receipt to Bill to Payment, with books and stock updated automatically.

Health

Trackers, treatments, medicines and cover for the whole family — pets included — ready the moment it matters.

  • Family records — One health profile per family member — history, conditions and documents, all in one place.
  • Tracker — Log vitals like blood pressure, glucose and weight, and see the trends over time.
  • Schedules — Medication and care schedules so nothing — and no one — gets missed.
  • Treatments — Track ongoing treatments, linked measurements and the medicines that go with them.
  • Vaccinations — Keep every family member vaccination record and see what is coming due.
  • Medicines — A running record of medicines, doses and refills for the whole family.
  • Menstruation — Track cycles simply and privately, with the insights that matter.
  • Pregnancy and moms diary — Follow a pregnancy week by week, with a private diary for the journey.
  • Insurance — Store health policies, premiums and claims, and never miss a renewal.
  • Lab-Report Scan — Snap a photo of a lab or blood report and AI reads every value — pick which to track and they save to the right family member.

Finance & Wealth

Wallets, assets, loans, budgets, tax and investments — a real balance sheet for your personal life and every business you run.

  • My wallet — All your accounts and balances in one place — a clear picture of where your money actually is.
  • Assets — Track what you own — property, vehicles, valuables — and their value over time.
  • Payables — Know what you owe and when it is due, so nothing catches you off guard.
  • Receivables — Track money owed to you and when it is expected to land.
  • Loans — Manage loans, EMIs and repayment schedules, with the interest and balance always clear.
  • Budgets — Set budgets by category and see where your money is really going each month.
  • Tax — Plan and organise your tax across countries, so filing season is not a scramble.
  • Investments — Track stocks, funds and other investments — cost, current value and returns at a glance.
  • Plans Investments — Model financial what-ifs, then compare scenarios side by side.
  • Month Ahead — A start-of-month briefing — what is coming in, what is going out, and exactly how much is safe to spend.
  • Expense Split — Dinners, trips and shared shopping — split the bill, see who owes what, and settle up without the arithmetic.
  • Transactions — Photograph a receipt and the amount, date and payee are read for you — then filed against the right account, loan or policy with the proof attached.

Personal Growth

Projects, goals, dreams and the things you keep meaning to start — with documentation, deadlines, budgets and progress you can actually see.

  • Learning — Courses, skills and study plans in one place — what you are learning, how far you have got, and what is next.
  • To-do — Personal, family, work, school or travel tasks — captured the moment they occur to you and sorted out later.
  • Exams — Every exam by school year, with topics to revise, preparation you can see, and results once they arrive.
  • Job Applications — Track every application from search to offer — interviews, contacts, documents and where each one stands.
  • Goals — Set goals with target dates and milestones, and watch your progress instead of guessing at it.
  • Wishlist — Anything you want to buy, do, learn, visit or achieve — written down before you forget it.
