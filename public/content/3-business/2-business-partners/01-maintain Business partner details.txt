🤝 Business Partners, Customers & Vendors — Keep Every Relationship Organized

🧑‍💼 Manage Your Customers — Maintain customer profiles with contact information, transactions, sales, invoices, receivables and payment history.
🚚 Manage Your Vendors — Keep supplier details, purchases, bills, payables, payments, contracts and important documents organized.
