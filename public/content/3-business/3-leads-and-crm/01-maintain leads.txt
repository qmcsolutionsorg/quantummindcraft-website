🎯 Sales CRM — Turn New Leads Into Customers
🧲 Capture Every New Lead — Add potential customers with their name, company, contact details, source, requirement and expected opportunity.
🔄 Track the Sales Journey — Move leads through your own stages such as New Lead → Contacted → Follow-up → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost.
✅ Manage Follow-ups & Tasks — Create calls, meetings, reminders, notes and next actions so no sales opportunity gets forgotten.
💰 Track Opportunities & Potential Revenue — Maintain expected deal value, probability, products/services and expected closing date to understand your sales pipeline.
🤝 Convert Lead to Customer — Once the deal is won, convert the lead into a Customer and continue with quotes, sales, invoices, receivables, payments, transactions and documents.

✨ Lead → Follow-up → Opportunity → Deal → Customer → Revenue — manage your complete sales journey in one place.
