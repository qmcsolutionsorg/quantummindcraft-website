📈 Lead Progress — From First Contact to Customer
🎯 Track Every Lead Stage — Follow each opportunity through New Lead → Contacted → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Won/Lost.
📅 Manage Meetings & Follow-ups — Record calls, meetings, appointments, next actions and reminders so no lead gets forgotten.
📝 Keep Complete Interaction History — Maintain notes, discussions, requirements, decisions and follow-up history for every lead.
📄 Keep Documents Connected — Attach proposals, quotations, presentations, agreements, emails and other important documents directly to the lead.
🤝 Convert Lead to Customer — When the deal is won, simply convert the lead into a Customer and carry important information forward for sales, invoices, receivables, payments and future business.

✨ Lead → Follow-up → Meeting → Documents → Deal → Customer — track the complete journey.
