🔄 End-to-End Sales Flow — From Order to Payment
🛒 Create Sales Order — Confirm the customer’s products/services, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, payment terms and delivery details.
📦 Fulfill & Deliver — Track product delivery or service completion, including partial deliveries, delivery dates and status.
🧾 Generate Customer Invoice — Create the invoice directly from the order/delivery, so you don’t have to enter the same information again.
💰 Track Payment & Receivable — Know exactly how much was invoiced, received, partially paid and still outstanding, including the receiving bank account.
🔗 Keep the Complete Journey Connected — See the entire history with quotation, order, delivery, invoice, payment, transactions, receipts and documents connected together.

✨ Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery/Service → Invoice → Receivable → Payment → Complete.
