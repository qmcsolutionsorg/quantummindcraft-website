🌟 Daily Habits — Build Better Routines for the Whole Family
✅ Create & Track Daily Habits — Maintain habits like brushing teeth, drinking water, reading, exercise, homework, sleep, healthy eating and screen-time limits.
📅 Record Every Day — Simply mark each habit as completed, missed or partially completed and build your daily history.
📊 See Your Success Rate — Automatically see daily, weekly and monthly completion percentages, streaks and progress for every habit.
💡 Get Help Choosing Good Habits — Pick from suggested habits for health, fitness, learning, productivity, self-care, finance and everyday routines instead of creating everything yourself.
🧒 Make It Kids-Friendly — Give kids simple age-appropriate habits, let them check off their own progress, build streaks and celebrate achievements—helping them learn consistency in a fun way.
