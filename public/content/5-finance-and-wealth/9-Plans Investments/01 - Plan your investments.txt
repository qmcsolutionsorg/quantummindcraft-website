🎯 Investment Planning — See Where Your Portfolio Is Going
💼 Start With Your Existing Investments — Keep your current stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, properties, crypto and other investments as the foundation of your plan.
➕ Plan New Investments — Add investments you’re considering without affecting your actual portfolio.
💰 Plan Future Contributions — Define how much you want to invest monthly, yearly or as a one-time investment.
📈 See Your Projection — Estimate how your portfolio could grow over 1, 5, 10, 20 years or longer using your chosen assumptions.
🔄 Compare Plan vs Actual — As you invest, move planned investments into your actual portfolio and continue tracking your real performance.
