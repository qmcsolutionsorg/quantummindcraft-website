🛒 Asset Buying Process — Plan Before You Buy
🎯 Define What You Want to Buy — Create the planned asset, set your target budget, expected purchase date and requirements.
🪜 Create Your Buying Process — Define every step you want to complete, such as research → compare → financing → insurance → inspection → negotiation → purchase.
💰 Plan Your Money — Decide how much you will pay from savings, down payment, trade-in or other available funds.
🏦 Plan Your Loan — Compare financing options, interest rates, EMI/monthly payments, loan terms and approvals before committing.
✅ Ready? Convert Plan to Asset — Once everything is ready, complete the purchase and convert the planned item into an Owned Asset, carrying its purchase, loan, insurance, documents and transaction information forward.

✨ Dream it → Plan it → Fund it → Finance it → Buy it → Track it.
