🏦 Loan Planning — Compare Before You Borrow
🎯 Plan Your Loan Before Applying — Create a planned loan with the amount you need, purpose, expected term and budget.
📋 Collect Multiple Loan Quotes — Maintain offers from different banks and lenders with interest rates, terms, fees and conditions.
⚖️ Compare Your Options — Compare monthly payments, interest rates, loan terms, total interest and overall repayment before making a decision.
✅ Finalize the Best Loan — Once you select an offer, convert the planned loan directly into an Actual Loan and continue tracking it.
🗑️ Keep Only What Matters — Delete or close loan plans you decide not to proceed with, keeping your financial records clean.

✨ Plan → Get Quotes → Compare → Decide → Convert to Actual Loan → Track.
