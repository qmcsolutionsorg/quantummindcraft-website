🌸 Menstruation & Cycle Tracking — Why It Matters
🩸 Track Your Period — Record start dates, duration, and flow intensity to understand your cycle better.
📅 Know What’s Coming — Estimate your next period, fertile window, and ovulation days so you can plan ahead.
😊 Understand Your Body — Track moods, symptoms, energy, and changes throughout different phases of your cycle.
📊 Discover Your Patterns — Compare cycle length, period duration, flow, and symptoms across months to spot changes over time.
💕 Keep Your Cycle History Together — Maintain your complete menstrual history in one place, making it easier to understand and discuss with your healthcare provider when needed.

✨ Track. Understand. Prepare. Stay connected with your body.
