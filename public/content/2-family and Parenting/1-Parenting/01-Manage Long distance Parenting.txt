👨‍👩‍👧 Long-Distance & Separated Parenting — Stay Connected Around Your Child

Manage important child information between parents in one organized place — school, health, activities, schedules, expenses, travel, emergency information and important contacts. 📄 Keep documents and records together, 💬 maintain parenting-related messages and discussions, and use an approval/request process for things like trips, activities, schedule changes or shared expenses. Maintain a clear history of requests, approvals, decisions and shared information so both parents can stay informed and focused on the child.

✨ Share → Discuss → Request → Approve → Coordinate → Stay connected.
