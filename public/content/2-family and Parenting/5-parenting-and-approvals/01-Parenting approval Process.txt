✅ Parenting Approval Process — Request, Decide & Follow Through
📤 Send for Approval — Create a request for travel, school activities, medical decisions, expenses, schedule changes, events, documents or other child-related matters.
📋 See All Approval Activities — Keep Pending, Approved, Rejected and Changes Requested items together with dates, messages, documents and decision history.
💬 Discuss Before Deciding — Parents can add comments, questions, supporting documents and important information directly to the request.
👍 Approve & Create the Action Plan — Once approved, automatically maintain the related tasks, appointments, payments, bookings, reminders and responsibilities.
📚 Keep the Complete History — Preserve Request → Discussion → Documents → Approval → Tasks → Completion for future reference.

✨ Ask → Discuss → Approve → Act → Complete — clear coordination around every important parenting decision.
