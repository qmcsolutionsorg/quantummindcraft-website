📁 Projects — Plan, Manage & Complete Every Project
🗂️ Create Different Types of Projects — Manage personal, business, home, school, travel, financial, event, renovation, learning or family projects separately.
🪜 Plan the Complete Project — Define objectives, phases, milestones, tasks, priorities, start dates, deadlines and project status.
👥 Share & Collaborate — Add family, friends, employees, partners or other people, assign responsibilities and track who is doing what.
💰 Plan & Track Your Budget — Set the project budget, record expenses and payments, attach receipts and compare budget vs actual cost.
📄 Keep Everything Connected — Maintain documents, photos, notes, meetings, tasks, expenses, reminders and important project information together.

✨ Create → Plan → Budget → Collaborate → Track → Complete — manage every project from idea to finish.
