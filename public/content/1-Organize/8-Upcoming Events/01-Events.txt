🎟️ Events — Discover, Plan & Never Miss What’s Happening
🔍 Browse Events Around You — Discover concerts, exhibitions, festivals, sports, shows, workshops, conferences and family/kids events.
➕ Add Events Manually — Found an event somewhere else? Simply enter the event name, date, time, venue, location, ticket details and other information.
❤️ Save What Interests You — Keep interesting events in your list even before deciding to attend, so you can come back to them later.
⏰ Never Miss an Event — Add events to your schedule and receive reminders for ticket sales, booking deadlines, event dates and start times.
🎫 Keep Everything Together — Once you decide to go, maintain tickets, bookings, expenses, documents, notes, friends/family attending and related plans with the event.
