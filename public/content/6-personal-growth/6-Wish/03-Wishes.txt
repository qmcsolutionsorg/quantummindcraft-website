🌟 My Wishes — Record Everything You Want to Do, Have or Experience
💭 Capture Every Wish — Save anything you wish to buy, do, learn, experience, visit or achieve before you forget it.
🛍️ Save Things From Stores — Found something you like? Keep the product, store, price, photo, link and notes in your wish list for later.
✈️ Not Just Things to Buy — Add wishes like travel somewhere, attend a concert, try an activity, learn a skill, visit a restaurant or do something with family.
📅 Turn a Wish Into a Plan — When you’re ready, add a budget, target date, savings goal, tasks and reminders and start working toward it.
❤️ Track Your Wish Journey — Move wishes through Wish → Planning → Doing → Completed, while keeping photos, documents, expenses and memories connected.

✨ See it → Wish it → Save it → Plan it → Do it → Remember it.
