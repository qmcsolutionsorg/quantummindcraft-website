🎯 Goals — Turn Every Dream Into a Trackable Plan
🌟 Create Any Type of Goal — Maintain short-term, long-term, financial, career, education, health, fitness, family, travel, business and personal goals.
📅 Define Your Target — Set the goal, target date, priority, target amount or measurable result you want to achieve.
🪜 Break Goals Into Action Steps — Divide bigger goals into milestones, tasks and activities so you know exactly what needs to happen next.
📊 Track Your Progress — Record achievements and see completion percentage, milestones reached, remaining work and whether you’re on track.
💰 Connect Goals With Your Life — Link financial goals with savings and investments, career goals with learning, health goals with habits, and other goals with related tasks, documents, expenses and reminders.

✨ Dream → Define → Plan → Take Action → Track Progress → Achieve.
